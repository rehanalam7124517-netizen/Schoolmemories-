Chapter 08 · Not The End

The OG Legends ❤️

We may never get the exact same classroom, the same benches, the same routine, or those random moments back in the same way. But the laughter, the jokes, the small arguments, the trips, the photos, and the videos will always be part of a chapter that was only ours.

Time will move on. We will go to different places, meet new people, and build new stories. Still, one old photo, one familiar name, or one moment can bring back an entire era in a second. That is why these memories matter — they remind us that we were here, together, for one unforgettable chapter.

This is not a goodbye. It is just the last page of this archive. The real story lives in every photo, every video, and every memory we still carry with us. Never the end of The OG Legends. ❤️♾️
